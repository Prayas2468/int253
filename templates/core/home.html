Inside the pipeline

The dataset is synthetic, the machine learning is real

A documented generator creates realistic land-acquisition records with meaningful relationships between ownership disputes, compensation progress, approvals, legal cases, clearance and delay outcomes. The target and future outcome fields are excluded from the model inputs to prevent leakage.
